# Mobile plan sheet

Effective 1 October 2026. This sheet is the only source for plan names, prices and features. Prices are in US dollars.

## Basic
Price: $9.99 per month
- 2 GB of data
- 100 minutes of calls
- 100 SMS
- A simple plan with no contract

## Standard
Price: $19.99 per month
- 15 GB of data
- Unlimited calls
- 500 SMS
- 5G included

## Unlimited
Price: $29.99 per month
- Unlimited data (fair use above 100 GB)
- Unlimited calls and SMS
- 5G included
- 5 GB of mobile hotspot

## Unlimited Plus
Price: $39.99 per month
- Unlimited data (fair use above 150 GB)
- 5G priority access on busy networks
- 20 GB of mobile hotspot
- One streaming subscription included

## Unlimited Max
Price: $54.99 per month
- Unlimited data with no fair use limit
- 5G priority access on busy networks
- 50 GB of mobile hotspot
- Two streaming subscriptions included
- International SMS included

## Family Data Plan
Price: $79.99 per month
- 200 GB of data shared by up to 5 lines
- Unlimited calls and SMS between family lines
- Each extra line costs $10 per month
- One bill for the whole family

## Family SMS Bundle
Price: $4.99 per month
- Add-on for the Family Data Plan
- Unlimited SMS for up to 5 family lines
- Data and calls are not included

## Student Saver
Price: $14.99 per month
- 20 GB of data
- Unlimited calls
- Valid student ID required
- ID is checked again every year

## Senior Basic
Price: $12.99 per month
- 5 GB of data
- 300 minutes of calls
- Priority customer support line

## Prepaid Daily Pass
Price: $1.99 per day
- 3 GB of data for each day you use
- No contract and no monthly fee
- You only pay on days you use it

## Business Starter
Price: $34.99 per line per month
- 50 GB of data for each line
- Minimum of 3 lines
- Invoice billing
- Priority business support

## Tablet Data Plan
Price: $14.99 per month
- 30 GB of data
- Data only, no calls
- For tablets and hotspots
