# Roaming policy

Effective 1 October 2026. A daily pass lets you use your plan abroad. Prices are in US dollars.

## Zone A: Neighbouring countries
Countries: Nepal, Bhutan, Sri Lanka, Bangladesh
Daily pass: $3 per day
- Roaming data: 1 GB for each day of the pass
- Calls cost $0.50 per minute
- SMS cost $0.10 each

## Zone B: Gulf and Middle East
Countries: United Arab Emirates, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Oman
Daily pass: $7 per day
- Roaming data: 2 GB for each day of the pass
- Calls cost $0.80 per minute
- SMS cost $0.15 each

## Zone C: Asia-Pacific
Countries: Singapore, Thailand, Japan, Australia
Daily pass: $9 per day
- Roaming data: 3 GB for each day of the pass
- Calls cost $0.90 per minute
- SMS cost $0.15 each

## Zone D: Europe and Americas
Countries: United Kingdom, France, Germany, United States, Canada
Daily pass: $12 per day
- Roaming data: 3 GB for each day of the pass
- Calls cost $1.20 per minute
- SMS cost $0.20 each

## Rules for all zones
- Activate a daily pass in the app before you travel
- A pass lasts 24 hours from the first use abroad
- Prepaid Daily Pass customers cannot buy roaming passes
- Passes are charged to your next monthly bill
